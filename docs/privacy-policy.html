Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 17, 2026  ·  Last updated: April 17, 2026  ·  Contact: support@slabstack.app

Slab Stack ("we", "our", or "us") is a Pokémon TCG portfolio tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have. We keep it plain — no legalese walls.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

  • Email address and display name (provided when you sign in via Google or email/password through Firebase Authentication).
  • A unique user ID generated by Firebase.

Portfolio data

  • Cards you add to your collection: card name, set, condition, purchase price, and any notes you enter.
  • Slab grades, grading company, and submission estimates you save.
  • Manually entered prices and price-check history.

Usage data

  • Basic app interaction logs (e.g. screens visited) collected automatically by Firebase Analytics, if enabled.
  • Crash reports collected by Firebase Crashlytics, if enabled.
  • We do not sell, rent, or broker your data to third parties.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To store and sync your card portfolio across devices.
  • To calculate ROI estimates, forecast projections, and submission breakdowns shown only to you.
  • To improve app stability and fix bugs.
  • To respond to support requests you send us.

We do not use your data for advertising profiling or sell it to data brokers.

3. Third-Party Services

Firebase (Google) — authentication, cloud database (Firestore), and optional analytics/crash reporting. Google's privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy
Pokémon TCG API (pokemontcg.io) — card metadata and images. No personal data is sent to this service. Their privacy policy: pokemontcg.io
eBay Finding API — used to look up recent sold prices for cards. Queries contain only card names and set identifiers — no personal information is ever sent to eBay. eBay's privacy policy: ebay.com privacy policy
PriceCharting — when you tap "Live Price Check" we open PriceCharting in your browser with a pre-filled search query. We do not share your account details with PriceCharting. PriceCharting privacy policy

4. Data Storage & Security

Your portfolio data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore, protected by Firebase Security Rules so only your authenticated account can read or write your own data.

All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS. We follow Firebase's recommended security practices and review rules regularly.

No security system is perfect. If you discover a vulnerability, please contact us at support@slabstack.app before disclosing it publicly.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated Firestore data is permanently deleted within 30 days.

6. Your Rights

You can at any time:

  • Access all data we hold about you by exporting your portfolio from the app.
  • Correct any data by editing it directly in the app.
  • Delete your account and all associated data from Settings → Account → Delete Account.
  • Request a copy of your data by emailing us.

EU/EEA residents may have additional rights under the GDPR. California residents may have additional rights under the CCPA. Contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Slab Stack is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. Significant changes will be notified in-app.
